## Claims a reviewer can attack

Each claim below is the argument for one group of hunks: what changed, from what to what, why, what it costs a run, which principle it serves, and what was cut.

**1. The P2 compile-cache guard prescribed a remedy that destroys a co-tenant's warm state, and left the verb that does it through.** Before: the guard refused the irreversible verbs on `/var/tmp/nki-intermediate-cache`, allowed `mv`, and told the seat "rename it aside instead"; `SKILL.md`'s P2 row and `agents/investigator.md` repeated the allowance. After: every destructive verb is refused on all four roots, with the guard's own reason (it cannot prove which root you own) and a remedy a seat can act on (report the case to the lead). Why: `references/artifact-layout.md` §4.10 and `references/measurement-pitfalls.md` scope rename-aside to a root you own, and that cache is defined as the opposite case — written outside every cache root a run can set, able to hold another tenant's kernels. Cost per run: one extra lead round on the rare legitimate clear, against the alternative of a seat following the guard's own instruction into a co-tenant's cache. Principle: a guard whose remedy causes the harm it guards is worse than no guard. Cut: the `IRREVERSIBLE` set and its comment, which existed only to carve out the `mv` allowance.

**2. The packaged skill failed the validator it ships against.** Before: 1096 characters against the 1024-character limit — the skill would not load. After: 1007 characters, with every trigger phrase, the `$vllm-neuron-parity:vllm-neuron-parity` manual-only invocation, and the whole eight-hook disclosure intact; the wording that carried no trigger is what went. Why: an unloadable skill makes every other fix in this release unreachable. Attack surface for a reviewer: check that no trigger phrase was lost — the diff is a rewording, not a shortening by deletion of content.

**3. Fourteen sites restated a duty their own sentence already cited.** The equivalence rule decided each one: a one-clause application in a file whose reader never loads the authority stays; a multi-clause restatement in a file whose reader is sent to the authority becomes a pointer; a role's own severity criterion stays. That is why `agents/adversarial-reviewer.md:19-28` keeps its illegibility criterion while its late §4.13 paragraph goes, and why the implementer keeps the block-diff hand-back protocol (this brief's own protocol, absent from §4.12) while losing §4.12's ledger-row field list. Cost per run: a seat reads one authority instead of choosing between two texts, one of which had already drifted — R13 and R15 are the two that had.

**4. The Codex mirrors carried four clauses their Markdown twins never had, and the four do not share one reason.** The `.md` is the authority for seat text, so the mirror cannot be the only place a duty lives. Three of the four told the seat when the LEAD would dispatch it: a decision the seat cannot act on, held by the lead, declared in the graph (`narrow_delta_scoped` is `evaluated_by: lead`), and already carried in `SKILL.md`. Those are cut, not copied into the `.md`, because copying them would have installed a second authority for the lead's own routing rule — and the reviewer's clause ends with a surface-naming duty its twin already states at `agents/adversarial-reviewer.md:62`, so nothing went with it. The fourth is a seat authoring duty, not a dispatch decision: the implementer's design record is updated in place on re-entry and its superseded lap material deleted. The graph node carries it verbatim (`workflow.pave.yaml:1354-1366`) and the lead's `SKILL.md:221` carries the lead's half, but the seat's standing authority is its own contract, and after the cut no document the seat loads carried it. So this one is **moved, not cut**: one clause in `agents/implementer.md` at `assemble_design_record`, pointing at the node's activity spec for the rule and at §4.12 for why a record that grows with lap count is a defect of that node. That is the A1 option taken; the alternative (rationale fix only) would have left the duty in the graph and the lead's copy alone. Verification is stronger than a marker check and stronger than the previous round of this patch: every mirror's `developer_instructions` is now rebuilt from its twin, so after the four harness substitutions each `.md` body is **byte-exact** inside its mirror, with the 115-word Codex header as the only other text (`validation-amendment.md` §3).

**5. Ten statements disagreed with the artifact they cite.** A wrong statement is worse than a missing one, because a seat that trusts it acts wrongly: the delegate install list omitted a delegate a campaign stage dispatches, so an operator would provision a session that cannot run a campaign; the patch-site count undercounted the port surface by two sites; `validate_pave.py` advertised a composition check that could never run, and the first fix for it (delete the call) replaced one false statement with another — the composition block would then have had no structural check at all, so the schema is vendored from pave-init and the call restored instead. The tenth is the one review round 1 caught unattributed: the agent table said the two Claude judges bind `fable`, which denies the rederiver's identical pin by exclusion, and dated the pin to "this release" when `VERSION`:249 and :307 record it at release 1.4.0. Each is now either correct or a pointer at the file that owns the fact.

**6. The ratchet pinned every prose file except the graph** (routed from the graph updater's every-copy search). `workflow.pave.yaml` carries the largest prose surface in the package — node purposes, activity prose, check rationale — and was the one document that could accrete with a green suite, which is exactly the defect class the ratchet exists to catch. Measure-then-set against the current ledger head: 2803 lines after revision 6, pinned at 3100, about 10% headroom, in line with README (+7%), implementer (+10%), and adversarial-reviewer (+10%). Cost per run: zero at run time; at landing time, a graph revision that needs more raises the pin in its own change and says why in `VERSION`, which is the discipline every other document already follows. Revision 6 shrank the graph by 8 lines while collapsing its copies to pointers, so the measurement is post-revision-6 and no coupling is left open.

**7. Every file that grows, grows for a stated reason.** `README.md` +8 lines (the seventh delegate with its P6 tie, the Model column's meaning, the vendored schema in the file tree); `skills/vllm-neuron-parity/hooks/compile-cache-guard.sh` +7 (the guard's reason and the remedy that replaces the wrong one — an unexplained refusal gets worked around); `references/artifact-layout.md` +4 (§4.12's missing settled-target-or-route bullet, so the investigator's pointer lands on a complete authority, plus the P2 root-count wording); `references/toolchain-evidence-pitfalls.md` +3 (the missing `**Why:**` line that file's own instrument requires); `tests/test_document_ceilings.py` +10 (the graph pin and the docstring clause saying why it exists); the three other guards +1 each (each cites where its enforcement rung is actually recorded); `scripts/validate_pave.py` +1. Against that, the six seat contracts and the lead skill shrink by 27 lines between them (investigator -9, lead skill -5, adjudicator, adversarial-reviewer, measurer and rederiver -3 each, implementer -1). Net across the 13 touched pinned documents is -13 lines and -250 words, and no existing ceiling is raised. Two files sit outside the pinned set and grow: `VERSION` +99 lines, which is the rewritten release entry and is append-only by class, and the new `references/pave-composition.schema.json` at 107 lines, which is machine input for the validator rather than prose a reader works from.
